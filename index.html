About

I am a statistician working on Bayesian methods for complex data. My PhD at Sapienza University of Rome, funded by a PNRR scholarship in collaboration with AGENAS, the Italian National Agency for Regional Healthcare Services, developed latent space models for dynamic and multiplex count networks, with patients moving between Italian local health authorities as the main application.

I spent over a year as a visiting researcher at Trinity College Dublin, and before the PhD I worked as a data scientist in industry. Since October 2026 I have been a Research Assistant at the UCD School of Medicine, working on scalable Bayesian methods for personalised immunotherapy in bladder cancer.

I write the software for the methods I develop, mostly in R and C++.

Research

Latent space models for networks

Directed, weighted networks observed over time and across several layers, with nodes placed in a latent space that explains who connects to whom.

Sparse count data

Hurdle negative binomial models for networks where most pairs exchange nothing and a few exchange a lot.

Clustering and hidden Markov models

Grouping the layers and time points of a multiplex network into regimes that share the same structure.

Bayesian computation

MCMC samplers written in C++, identifiability through Procrustes alignment, and methods that scale to larger data.

Healthcare mobility

Where patients travel for surgery in Italy, what drives it, and how the pattern changes from year to year.

Biomedical applications

Bayesian modelling for personalised immunotherapy in bladder cancer, my current project at UCD.

Software

countLSM

Dynamic latent space models for directed count networks with a hurdle negative binomial likelihood. Eight model variants, with sender and receiver effects, geographical distance and an exposure offset. Reproduces the results of the JRSS C paper.

R, C++ (RcppArmadillo), Metropolis-within-Gibbs

countLSHMM

A latent space hidden Markov model for longitudinal multilayer count networks. Each layer and time point belongs to a regime with its own latent space; the number of regimes is chosen from the realigned DIC, regime occupancy and agreement between chains.

R, C++ (RcppArmadillo), forward-filtering backward-sampling, adaptive Metropolis-Hastings
